# Hearthlands

A relaxing medieval kingdom builder set on a real Earth of **8 million isometric tiles** (2000 × 4000).
Pick any spot on the planet, then grow from a hamlet into a kingdom at your own pace. There are no timers and you can pause whenever you like, but neighbouring kingdoms may raid your towns.

## Features
- **Real Earth**: continents and coastlines come from Natural Earth country shapes. Mountains, deserts,
  rainforests, taiga, tundra and ice follow their real locations. Every tile knows which country it's in.
- **Pick any homeland**: the start screen rates the woodland, stone, farmland and coast around the spot you choose.
- **Mixed building**: paint Housing, Farmland, Pasture (sheep and cows, food and a little wool), Woodlot (planted timber), Park (a happier town), Workshop and Shops zones, and villagers build them over time.
  Place key buildings yourself: Lumber Camp, Quarry, Iron Mine, Fishing Dock, Watchtower and Market.
- **Expansion**: your border grows with your town, and Watchtowers push it further. Found new settlements anywhere on Earth.
- **Peaceful neighbours**: about 600 villages around the world. Trade with them and send gifts,
  and close friends can join your kingdom.
- **Progression**: Hamlet → Village → Town → City → Kingdom, 19 gentle goals, and 5 Wonders.
- **Cozy feel**: villagers walk around your towns, the capital's keep grows with your tier, generative music
  plays alongside birdsong, and building gives a little vibration.
- **A slow, calm pace** meant to be enjoyed over days: Village takes about 30 minutes of play, Town about 2 hours, City about 5 and Kingdom about 10. There is play, double speed and pause, and nothing happens while the app is closed.
- **Pip the messenger bird** guides your first few steps: zoning fields and homes, then building a Lumber Camp. You can skip the guide at any time.
- **Three save slots**, so you can keep realms in different places on Earth. Each autosaves on the device.
- Goals are tucked into a 📜 scroll button that shows a badge when you finish one.
- **A living world**: a gentle day and night cycle about every 5 minutes of play, with window glow in town at night. Seasons follow each hemisphere. Snow spreads and melts tile by tile, woods turn gold in autumn, and blossom opens in spring. Fields rest in winter and give a harvest boost in late summer and autumn.
- **Wildlife and weather**: sheep, cows and chickens on the farms. Deer, reindeer, rabbits, gazelles and camels in the wild. Flocks of birds, fishing boats and sailing ships. Drifting clouds with shadows, showers with soft rain sound, snowfall in winter, and crickets at night.
- **Regional building styles**: towns build in the style of where they stand on Earth (timber-framed, Nordic, Mediterranean, adobe, tropical stilt houses or curved-eave). Homes improve by themselves from cottage to town house to manor as a town grows, and each step holds more people.
- **Neighbours with personality**: each village has a leader with a temperament. Leaders send letters with small requests (help is rewarded, ignoring them costs nothing), share little stories with friends, and their villages visibly grow on the map.
- **Town mood**: everyday buildings (well, apiary, windmill, chapel, tavern, school), goods and charm make towns 🙂 Content, 😊 Happy or 😄 Joyful. Happier towns attract families faster, and mood never punishes.
- **Goods**: bread (bakery), tools (smithy), cloth (weaver) and ale or wine (brewery). Each is a simple one-step workshop, and the goods row appears once goods unlock.
- **Regional specialties**: orchards in timber-framed towns, ice-fishing huts in Nordic ones, vineyards in Mediterranean ones, oasis gardens in adobe towns, cacao groves in the tropics and rice terraces in curved-eave towns.
- **Villagers with lives**: everyone has a name from their region, an age, a family, a home, a job and a growing skill. Weddings, babies, coming of age, master craftspeople, retirements and young families moving between towns all appear in each town's chronicle. Tap anyone walking about to meet them.
- **Rivers and bridges**: 44 great rivers follow their real courses (Nile, Amazon, Mississippi, Rhine, Danube, Volga, Yangtze, Ganges and more). Docks can be built on a riverbank, riverside farms get a fertile-floodplain bonus, and wooden bridges appear wherever a road crosses a river.
- **Pets**: many families keep a dog or a cat. Dogs trot along behind their owners, cats doze on doorsteps, and each villager's card shows the family pet.
- **Market days and visitors**: every 8 days, towns with a Market hold a market day. Covered wagons and travellers come in from the neighbouring villages, bringing gold and goodwill, and travellers also stop by the tavern.
- **Builder crews**: each town starts with one crew and gains one more per 150 villagers. A **Builder's Yard** adds two crews, so more zoned tiles go up at once, and makes building there 25% faster. Builders can be seen hammering away at every construction site.
- **A world that feels alive**:
  - Little scenes: children playing tag, elders on benches, people fishing by rivers, neighbours chatting at the market.
  - Emotes over villagers (♥ ♪ 💤), chimney smoke and laundry flapping in the wind.
  - Water: waves lapping the shore, ducks and swans, jumping fish and whales far out at sea.
  - Wildlife: deer herds that bound away, and birds that take off when you tap the trees.
  - Seasons: blossom petals, autumn leaves, snowmen and summer swimmers.
  - Light and weather: golden hour and blue hour, long dawn and dusk shadows, morning fog, thunderstorms followed by rainbows, desert heat shimmer.
  - Nights: fireflies, lantern-carrying walkers, flickering windows and moonlight on the water.
  - Neighbour villages with smoke, folk and sheep, and caravans and pilgrims travelling between them.
  - Sound that follows the view: waves, rivers, town bustle, forest birds, owls and thunder.
  - A **Gentle visuals** setting turns the extra effects down, and **Always daytime** and **Always fair weather** switch off night and weather.
- **Explore and connect**: send explorers to 30 natural wonders of the world for a journal of discoveries. Highways link your towns with carts travelling them. A **Shipyard** adds a fishing fleet and ferries for short sea crossings. Mines and quarries turn up rare finds (silver, gold, gems, fossils, geodes).
- **People and stories**: seasonal **festivals** (Blossom Day, Midsummer Fair, Harvest Fair, Midwinter Lights), small **requests** from named villagers (find a lost goat, build a well, help a family), and a **royal family** whose ruler marries, has heirs and passes on the crown, with portraits and titles that grow with the realm.
- **Keeping your realm**: back up a realm as a file or code and restore it from the main menu. **Trophies** are earned across all realms. **Realm history** shows charts of villagers and stores, plus a timeline of milestones.
- **Gentle challenge**: bigger buildings have a small daily **upkeep**. If it can't be paid they simply rest (💤) and wake up again when it can, so nothing ever breaks. From Village tier each town can master one **speciality** (🌾 Farming, ⚒ Crafts, 💰 Trade, ⛏ Industry or 📖 Learning): +30% to that trade and −10% to the town's other work, so you plan which town does what.
- **Regional wonders**: Cliff Monastery, Great Bridge, Observatory and Botanical Dome.
- **The late game**: build a **Harbour** to send settlers to other continents. Chart **sea routes** between harbour towns on different continents (ships sail them, each adds gold and room for another settlement). Make towns lovelier with **civic improvements**: town green, paved streets, fountain square, street lanterns and window boxes. Long-term goals include settling six continents.
- **Grand buildings and handy tools**: wonders and markets cover a 2×2 patch. Buildings can be turned around while placing, and moved or turned later for free. An **Undo** button takes back the last zone or road strokes. Family homes grow fences, vegetable patches and sheds as they settle in.
- **Mining zone**: pits on rocky ground for stone and a little iron. Big towns name their quarters (Old Town, Riverside, Market Quarter and more).
- **Getting about**: dig **canals** that boats use (docks and farms count them as river), build **stone bridges** across rivers and **tunnels** through mountains, and send **caravans** between your own towns. Villagers walk faster on roads, and footpaths wear into the grass where they walk most.
- **Villagers**: rename villagers and pets, follow anyone with the **follow camera**, browse **family trees**, and watch work clothes show each trade (with a golden sash for masters). Children walk to school and often take up a parent's trade.
- **More goods**: pottery, glass, spices and books, each with a small, gentle benefit. Trade prices change with the seasons. Past Kingdom lies the **Empire** and a **golden age**, and the realm's **prestige** grows with everything you do. Each town hall shows its figures (homes, jobs, daily output).
- **A wider world**: peaceful **neighbouring kingdoms** slowly grow across the map, and great **migrations** cross the continents (birds heading south in autumn, the Serengeti herds, caribou on the tundra). Hold a **Travelling Fair**, a **Grand Wedding** or a **Grand Tournament**, and friendly leaders pay **royal visits** with gifts.
- **Four more building styles**: Slavic log houses, Indian havelis, Andean stone houses and domed Arabic homes.
- **Living details**: windmill sails, water wheels, a swinging tavern sign and smithy sparks. At night the stars and the moon, in its current phase, reflect in the water. Music changes with the region and the season, villagers chat in busy towns, and chapel bells ring at noon and for weddings.
- **Research and talents**: a **research tree** of 16 ideas across five eras (crop rotation to golden harmony), studied with knowledge from schools, scriptoria, the library and the observatory. A **talent tree** with five branches (Steward, Builder, Merchant, Patron, Explorer), with points earned from tiers, wonders and prestige.
- **Worker skills**: every trade has its own skill (Green Thumb for farmers, Felling for woodcutters, Forging for smiths and so on) that grows the longer someone does that job and makes that work better. **Know-how** grows in any job and helps with everything. Skills stay with a person when they change jobs, and towns put their most experienced folk in each job.
- **Double speed**: pause, play or 2× speed.
- **Progression**: goals come in seven **chapters** (A Humble Start to Kingdom and Beyond), each with its own reward. The research tree has **40 ideas** over six eras, and some ideas unlock buildings (Grist Mill, Warehouse, Potter, Glassworks, Spice House, Scriptorium). Tap the 🏆 ring by the realm name for the **Progress** page. Reaching a new tier brings fireworks, a fanfare and a card showing everything it unlocked.
- **Economy**: gentle **storage limits**, raised by Granaries and Warehouses; when a store is full, the extra is sold for a little gold. Two-step **production chains** (grain → flour → bread, wool → cloth). Towns earn **fame titles** for what they make most (Bakers' Town, Famous Wine Town…), each with +10%. Friendly neighbours offer year-long **contracts** with big rewards.
- **Kingdoms and war**: the kingdoms on the map are alive. They grow, take in villages, and conquer each other. From Village tier they send **raiders** who march on your towns (you get a warning and see them coming). Hold them off and they leave gold behind; let them through and they carry off supplies, and a strong enough raid can capture an outlying town (never your capital). Build **Barracks** to train garrisons, add **Watchtowers** and **Stone Forts**, research Drill, Fortifications and Tactics, and raise a **field army** to defend towns or take enemy villages (taking a kingdom's capital breaks it apart). You can also pay **tribute** for a year of peace.
- **Rulers and years**: every ruler is born with a gift and chooses more as their reign goes on (Wise, Master Builder, Diplomat, Beloved…). Each new year brings a cosy **year in review**.
- **Satisfying touches**: workplaces level up by themselves with time and skill, with a pennant to show it. Buildings pop up when finished, with hammer taps and a chime. Workers carry sacks, logs and fish, and carts haul goods to the town hall. Towns get grander with each tier: paved squares, banners, stone yard walls and a founder's statue.
- **Roads** form by themselves from each building to its town hall, and villagers walk along them. The **Road** tool in Zones lets you lay extra roads for free. Roads curve smoothly round bends, with grassy verges and worn tracks, and turn to cobbles in cities.
- **Sound settings**: separate volume sliders for master, music, nature and town sounds, rain and storms, and sound effects.
- Works in portrait and landscape.

## Tuned for the Galaxy S26 Ultra
- Renders at up to 3× pixel density, with art drawn at 3× so it stays crisp on the QHD+ screen. Runs at the full 120 Hz refresh rate.
- **S Pen**: hover to see a tile's name. In Zones, the pen paints and fingers move the map. Hold the side button to erase.
- Immersive full screen (system bars hidden, swipe to peek). Landscape controls stay clear of the punch-hole camera.
- The Back gesture closes menus and modes. With nothing open, it sends the game to the background instead of quitting.

## Play in a browser
```
npm install
npm run dev
```
Then open http://localhost:8080. It's plain HTML and JS with no build step.

## Developing
- `npm run lint` checks the code with ESLint, and `npm test` plays the game in a real browser (Playwright).
  `npm run check` runs both, and GitHub Actions runs them on every push before building the APK.
- See **[ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md)** for how the game fits together, and step-by-step recipes for
  adding buildings, zones, research, talents, whole new systems, and save changes.

## Android APK
Every push runs `.github/workflows/android.yml`, which wraps `web/` with Capacitor and builds a debug APK.
Download it from the run's **Artifacts** section (`Hearthlands-apk`), then install it on your phone
(allow "install unknown apps" if Android asks).

Local build (needs the Android SDK and JDK 21):
```
npm install
npx cap add android
npx capacitor-assets generate --android
npx cap sync android
cd android && ./gradlew assembleDebug
```
